## may 24, 2026

### idea #1 — 5:30am

**raw thought:** the roles of PM and designer are blurring fast. if a PM has access to a trained agent with a design system already set up (layer 1, 2, 3), and basic UX instincts, do they still need a designer? and from the designer's side — the handoff is changing. it used to be high-fidelity designs handed to devs. now it's starting to include .md files, maybe even front-end code. open-source design systems from Apple and Material are already being distributed as .md files, which makes it trivially easy to bootstrap front-end work and pass it into Claude or Lovable. the deeper question: will designers get hired as consultants who set up design systems once — especially for B2B products where design is functional, not emotional? there's also a feature idea in here — a side-by-side AI workflow where Claude acts as the head of product/design (thinking partner, reviewing screenshots) while Claude Code is the intern executing. and further: what if Figma had a Claude canvas layer on top of it — so as you design, it builds in parallel, and can even suggest alternate versions? code is just data. why can't design be the input form instead of words?

**seed angle:** the designer's job isn't disappearing — it's splitting into two things: setting up the system once, and knowing what to ask for next.

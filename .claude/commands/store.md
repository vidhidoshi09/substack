Store a new Substack idea into the ideas bank.

The user's raw thought is: $ARGUMENTS

## What to do

1. Read `instructions.md` in full.
2. Read `store.md`.
3. Get today's date and current time.
4. If the last entry's date differs from today, add a day divider first:
   ```
   ⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯⎯
   ## <month day, year>
   ```
   If same day, continue under the existing heading.
5. Append the new entry:
   ```
   ### idea #<N> — <hh:mm am/pm>

   **raw thought:**
   <preserved thought>

   **seed angle:** <one sharp sentence on what this could become>

   ---
   ```
6. Confirm: "✅ idea #N saved. type `/thought` to see your bank, or `/post <number>` to draft it."

## rules for the raw thought

follow the store.md formatting rules in `instructions.md` exactly:
- keep original length. do not compress or cut.
- keep original structure. pointers stay pointers. stream stays stream.
- no em dashes. ever.
- fix only typos and speech-to-text artifacts. do not rephrase.
- lowercase throughout.

Do NOT generate a post.

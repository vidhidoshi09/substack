Store a new Substack idea into the ideas bank.

The user's raw thought is: $ARGUMENTS

## What to do

1. Read the current `ideas.md` file.
2. Append a new entry at the bottom in this exact format:

```
### Idea #<N> — <today's date>

**Raw thought:** <the user's input, lightly cleaned up but not rewritten>

**Seed angle (1 sentence):** <a single sharp angle or hook that this idea could become>

---
```

Where `<N>` is the next sequential number based on existing entries.

3. Confirm to the user: "✅ Idea #N saved. Type `/thought` to see your bank, or `/post <idea number or topic>` to draft it."

Do NOT generate a full post. Just capture and lightly sharpen the seed.
